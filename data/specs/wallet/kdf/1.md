title: Credential Discovery
level: REQUIRED
requirements:
  - number: 1
    level: RECOMMENDED
    description: >
      The credential wallet should host the credential offering endpoint that the credential issuer should call to share the [credential offer](/terminology#credential-offer)
    operationId: credential-offer
  - number: 2
    level: REQUIRED
    description: >
      The credential issuer MUST expose an endpoint that provides relevant information to ensure a convenient and secure credential issuance.
    operationId: get-issuer-well-known
---
A wallet holder who intends to receive credentials from an issuer should be able to get relevant information on how to obtain the credentials, so that, the process of credential issuance is convenient and secure.
